Founder · Nonprofit · Product + engineering2025 – now

WatanHub

A nonprofit mentorship platform that pairs Afghan students with volunteer mentors for college, scholarships, and careers.

Problem
For young Afghans, many of them new to the US, advice on applications, scholarships, and careers depends on who you already know. Most don’t know anyone who has done it.
Key decision
Measure outcomes, not sign-ups. The platform follows admissions, scholarships, and jobs over years, so we can learn which kind of mentorship actually helps, and for whom.
What I built
Mentor applications and review, student–mentor matching that respects each mentor’s workload, cohort management, mentor dashboards, outcome tracking, and verifiable certificates, in English, Dari, and Pashto.

Founder · Product + engineering2025 – now

CodeSparring

An AI interviewer for practicing technical interviews out loud, live at codesparring.dev.

Problem
Prep tools grade your code. Real interviews grade how you think out loud: explaining trade-offs, debugging aloud, recovering from a wrong turn.
Key decision
Hints diagnose before they help. A LangGraph agent works out whether you’re stuck on the concept, the approach, the implementation, or a bug, so the AI guides without handing over the answer.
What I built
A phase-aware interviewer with retrieval over problems and your history, in-browser code runs against tests, FSRS spaced review, voice mode, and Stripe billing.
